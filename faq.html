FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTION

How do I enroll in a course?

Open any course card and select Enroll. If you don't have an account yet, you'll be asked to register first.

Are courses self-paced?

Most courses run on a weekly schedule with recorded sessions, so you can catch up if you miss a live class.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of enrolling if you haven't completed more than one module.
